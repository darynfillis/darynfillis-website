Primary insight

This looks less like a single-agent relationship and more like a team-level lender relationship inside Stephanie Younger Group.

Unit-count implication

With 26 of 72 units, SYG appears to be a repeatable source of transaction flow, not a one-off high-ticket anomaly.

Average-loan implication

SYG average loan amount is approximately $1,414,367, which is above the full dataset average of $1,167,886.

Recommended Leadership Narrative

“This is a 100-loan, $109.7 million production picture. It is not just a volume opportunity. It is a unit-flow and high-average-loan-amount opportunity. Stephanie Younger Group appears to represent meaningful production dollars, recurring referral activity, and stronger-than-average deal size.”

The strategic path is not to tell agents that we are better at mortgages. The stronger approach is to show where they are leaving repeat, referral, listing-side, and move-up business on the table.
