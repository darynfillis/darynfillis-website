Move-up strategy 23 minutes + Q&A For buyer and listing agents

The move-up method.

Homeowners with equity can still feel trapped by a low rate, a sell-first assumption, or an incomplete view of the numbers. This session gives your agents a clear framework for coordinating the sale, the next purchase, and the financing as one move.

  • Move from the rate question to the full decision
  • Audit equity, debt, and buying power together
  • Compare sell-first, buy-first, and concurrent paths
  • Use credits only when the tradeoffs fit
