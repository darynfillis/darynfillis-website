Step 1

Set your hourly filter.

The Excel formulas are mirrored in the browser: total hours are non-empty 30-minute activity blocks divided by two, green hours are G-coded blocks divided by two, and green percentage is green hours divided by total logged hours.

Green means high ROI.

You would pay your target hourly rate or more for this work because it drives production, referrals, strategy, or scalable growth.

Red means low ROI.

You would not pay your target hourly rate for this work. It should be delegated, systemized, batched, or avoided where possible.

ROE means return on energy.

Low drains you, neutral is steady, and high gives energy back. That energy rating feeds the Genius Grid.

Step 3

Review your Genius Grid.

The grid auto-populates from the transcript logic. Red items move below the line based on results. Green items move into Competence, Excellence, or Genius based on energy. Drag any card into a different zone when your judgment is more accurate.
