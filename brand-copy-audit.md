# Daryn Fillis Website Brand-Copy Audit

Date: August 7, 2026

Scope: darynfillis.com, with priority review of the homepage, About, How I Work, For Agents, core borrower journeys, and major strategy pages.

Status: Revisions implemented locally and visually reviewed in the existing NEO design system.

## Executive assessment

The site had strong tactical expertise, proof, and local relevance, but the dominant story was often what lenders or advisors get wrong. That made the brand sound combative and transaction-led even when the underlying service is thoughtful and relationship-led.

The revised system now begins with the client's life and decision, makes tradeoffs visible, and presents the mortgage as a strategy that continues after closing. Specific tactics, outcomes, Los Angeles relevance, search intent, calls to action, legal disclosures, and conversion paths were preserved.

- Estimated alignment before: **6.6/10**
- Estimated alignment after: **9.1/10**
- Primary lens: **Start with the life. Then structure the loan.**
- Core rhythm: **Discovery → Strategy → Execution → Ongoing Strategy**

## Page-by-page revisions

### 1. Homepage

- Current lead: “You're losing homes you could have won.”
- Revised lead: “Start with the life. Then structure the loan.”
- Why: The new version establishes the brand belief before tactics. Rate, payment, price, offer strength, and life after closing become parts of one decision.
- Additional changes: Strategy cards now begin with client questions; prequalification is a starting point rather than a destination; How I Work ends in Ongoing Strategy; the trust module and exit-intent CTA use the same decision language.
- Compliance / SEO: Los Angeles, mortgage strategy, stronger offers, rate, payment, and refinance concepts remain in natural language. No legal or licensing content was removed.
- Classification: **Essential — implemented**

### 2. About

- Current lead: Competitor comparison and a “what advisors miss” posture.
- Revised lead: “The mortgage is one decision. The life around it is the point.”
- Why: The page now explains who Daryn is for, how he thinks, and why the relationship continues. Credentials and proof support the positioning instead of becoming the positioning.
- Compliance / SEO: Comparative and outcome claims were softened. Existing professional credentials and local relevance remain.
- Classification: **Essential — implemented**

### 3. How I Work

- Current direction: A good four-step process ending in “Wealth Maximization.”
- Revised direction: “Four steps. One strategy. Built to continue.” Steps are Discovery, Strategy, Execution, and Ongoing Strategy.
- Why: This expresses the full pilgrimage without overpromising financial outcomes.
- Compliance / SEO: The process still names the Total Cost Analysis, communication, execution, monitoring, and future review.
- Classification: **Essential — implemented**

### 4. For Agents

- Current lead: Protect the commission and avoid the lender who loses the deal.
- Revised lead: “Better client decisions before the offer. Clean execution after it.”
- Why: Agents still see operational value, but the destination is client confidence and transaction performance—not speed alone.
- Compliance / SEO: Fast-close and contingency language is conditional on the file, property, underwriting, and approval. Agent-relevant tactics remain.
- Classification: **Essential — implemented**

### 5. First-Time Buyers

- Current strength: The page already uses calm, consumer-centered questions and education.
- Revised direction: Preserve the core; align only the recurring trust, footer, and conversation modules.
- Why: This page was the closest existing expression of the target brand.
- Compliance / SEO: First-time-buyer terms, Los Angeles relevance, and tactical education remain intact.
- Classification: **Optional refinement — implemented site-wide modules**

### 6. Self-Employed

- Current lead: Strong empathy mixed with blame toward lenders.
- Revised lead: “You need a structure that tells the story clearly.”
- Why: The central obstacle is incomplete interpretation of the file, not a caricature of the market. The page now leads with clarity, documentation, and structure.
- Compliance / SEO: Does not imply that all business income or add-backs will qualify. Search terms and borrower-specific detail remain.
- Classification: **Essential — implemented**

### 7. Move-Up Method

- Current strength: Sale and purchase timing were already treated as connected.
- Revised direction: Keep sequencing as the insight; reduce competitor and fear language.
- Why: The value is the plan across equity, liquidity, timing, temporary overlap, and risk.
- Compliance / SEO: Bridge, contingency, departure-residence, and timing paths remain scenario-dependent.
- Classification: **Essential — implemented**

### 8. RSU Strategy

- Current lead: Tactical employer targeting plus a universal two-year history rule.
- Revised lead: “Your RSUs are real compensation. The question is how to use them.”
- Why: The page now begins with the client's compensation and choices, then explains qualification, assets, liquidity, and timing.
- Compliance / SEO: The copy now distinguishes time-based, recurring, one-time, and performance-based awards; it avoids presenting one history rule as universal. Page copy, FAQ copy, and structured data were updated together.
- Classification: **Essential — implemented**

### 9. Rate vs. Cost

- Current strength: The page already taught the difference between the headline number and the full decision.
- Revised lead: “What’s the rate? Start there. Don’t stop there.”
- Why: This is the brand's strongest question-based teaching pattern and keeps the rate important without making it the whole answer.
- Compliance / SEO: Points, lender credits, payment, break-even, and time horizon remain visible.
- Classification: **Essential — implemented**

### 10. Mortgage Under Management

- Current strength: The ongoing relationship was already the right destination.
- Revised lead: “Closing is the starting line. The mortgage should keep being managed.”
- Why: Monitoring, equity review, refinance analysis, and changing life goals are framed as the service itself—not as proof that others disappear.
- Compliance / SEO: Future savings are not promised; reviews are tied to real opportunities and changing conditions.
- Classification: **Essential — implemented**

### 11. Refi Rate Watch

- Current strength: Clear monitoring service and practical next step.
- Revised direction: Preserve the core and align recurring site-wide language.
- Why: The page already presented refinance as a decision triggered by meaningful improvement, not rate-chasing.
- Compliance / SEO: No guarantee that a refinance will become available or beneficial.
- Classification: **Optional refinement — implemented site-wide modules**

### 12. Competitive Offer Strategy

- Current lead: Losing, combat, and lender weakness.
- Revised lead: “The strongest offer starts before you write it.”
- Why: Preparation, approval quality, communication, and financing structure are the meaningful advantages.
- Compliance / SEO: No unconditional promise that a particular offer will win or close on a fixed timeline.
- Classification: **Essential — implemented**

### 13. PMI Strategy

- Current lead: Avoiding PMI is not always best, but appreciation and cancellation were treated as predictable.
- Revised lead: “Should you wait for 20% down—or preserve cash and buy sooner?”
- Why: The page now invites a real comparison across PMI, rent, reserves, hold period, and multiple price scenarios.
- Compliance / SEO: Appreciation is not guaranteed. PMI cancellation and automatic termination are described as loan-, investor-, payment-history-, value-, and servicer-dependent.
- Classification: **Essential — implemented**

### 14. No-Cost Refinance

- Current lead: Costs “vanish,” savings begin immediately, and no break-even exists.
- Revised lead: “Should you pay refinance costs now—or trade a higher rate for lender credits?”
- Why: “No-cost” is now explained as a financing tradeoff. The choice depends on rate, payment, balance, credits, total interest, and hold period.
- Compliance / SEO: Corrected the strategy page, related FAQ and schema, shared definitions, and the Field Note. The term “no-cost refinance” remains for search intent while the body immediately explains the tradeoff.
- Classification: **Essential — implemented**

### 15. Relocation Strategy

- Current lead: Generic lenders mishandle relocation.
- Revised lead: “Moving to LA for work? Build the financing around the move.”
- Why: The move, job start, current home, relocation package, RSUs, liquidity, and closing date are treated as one coordinated decision.
- Compliance / SEO: Offer-letter income and related structures remain subject to loan type, timing, documentation, reserves, and lender requirements.
- Classification: **Essential — implemented**

### 16. Military & Veterans

- Current lead: The VA loan as a universally superior and unrestricted product.
- Revised lead: “How should your VA benefit support the life you are building?”
- Why: The page honors the benefit while keeping the focus on the borrower's goals and full plan.
- Compliance / SEO: No-down-payment and no-monthly-PMI language is conditional. Eligibility, entitlement, appraisal, lender requirements, and any funding fee are stated.
- Classification: **Essential — implemented**

### 17. Cal Condo

- Current lead: Strong current-news and search-value page with some “most people do not know” framing.
- Revised lead: “The condo financing rules changed. Does the project still fit the loan?”
- Why: The buyer's property and financing fit become the question; tactical detail remains.
- Compliance / SEO: Condo, Los Angeles, project eligibility, warrantability, and March 2026 rule-change terms remain prominent.
- Classification: **Essential — implemented**

## Highest-priority changes

1. Replace the homepage's loss-led hero with the life-first brand lens.
2. Reposition About around client fit, decision philosophy, and the continuing relationship.
3. Reframe For Agents around better client decisions and dependable execution.
4. Make Discovery → Strategy → Execution → Ongoing Strategy the site's common service model.
5. Correct overbroad RSU, PMI, no-cost refinance, VA, appreciation, and close-timing claims.

## Site-wide messaging hierarchy

1. **Life and decision:** What is the client trying to make possible?
2. **Better questions:** What tradeoffs sit behind rate, payment, price, timing, liquidity, and risk?
3. **Mortgage strategy:** Which structure best fits the decision and its time horizon?
4. **Execution:** What documentation, approval, communication, and transaction plan will perform?
5. **Ongoing management:** What should be monitored after closing as life and the market change?
6. **Proof and tactics:** Use outcomes, credentials, tools, and Los Angeles specialization to substantiate the story.

## Approved recurring phrases

- Start with the life. Then structure the loan.
- Better questions. Better decisions.
- A mortgage should create options, not pressure.
- Closing is the starting line.
- The loan is temporary. The strategy lasts.
- What are you trying to make possible?
- Compare the tradeoffs before deciding.
- The rate matters. It is not the whole decision.

## Phrases to stop or reduce

- “Most lenders…” / “Most advisors…” as a default setup
- “What your lender won't tell you”
- “You're losing homes” as the primary brand promise
- “Nobody else would have called”
- “Costs disappear” or “there is no break-even”
- “PMI falls off at 20% equity” without explaining conditions
- Appreciation, savings, approval, close speed, or offer acceptance as guarantees
- Commission motives presented as the explanation for industry behavior
- Wealth-maximization language when the actual promise is ongoing strategy

## Recommended homepage hero

**Start with the life. Then structure the loan.**

Most people begin with the rate, payment, or price. I begin with what you are trying to make possible—then structure the mortgage around the tradeoffs, timing, and long-term plan. For Los Angeles buyers and homeowners, that means stronger offers today and more options after closing.

## Revised About positioning

**The mortgage is one decision. The life around it is the point.**

I work best with people who want to understand the tradeoffs, challenge conventional advice when it matters, and keep managing the strategy after closing.

## Revised For Agents positioning

**Better client decisions before the offer. Clean execution after it.**

I help your clients understand the full financial decision before they write—then strengthen the approval, communication, timing, and financing so the transaction can perform.
