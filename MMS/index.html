01 · V1 SOLUTION

Serve NEO first, then scale through a disciplined development pipeline

MMS is in MVP V1 and is rolling out across NEO. NEO is the active proof environment. The first release remains focused on adoption, measurable value, and the core mortgage planning, client-experience, presentation, partner, and workflow capabilities of the platform. A successful NEO rollout also strengthens the NEO recruiting proposition by giving prospective Mortgage Advisors a differentiated operating platform.

The vision began with Vishal's ask: use AI and internal development to replace or reduce dependence on third-party vendors. That is a real pain point for NEO Mortgage Advisors, who carry fragmented tools, recurring software expense, and disconnected workflows.

Drawing on Daryn's expertise in client experience inside and outside mortgage, MMS turns that direction into a usable interface and operating experience. The UI was created to execute on the vision: make the work clearer for Mortgage Advisors, make the client journey more consistent, and give Better a platform it can continue to improve.

Tinman runs the core workflow. MMS improves the experience. MMS is not intended to replace Tinman. It enhances Tinman data and workflow to help Mortgage Advisors deliver the best possible consumer experience.

V1 does not include a full CRM. Landing pages, calculators, presentations, and other MMS experiences should capture prospect information into MMS and transmit it to existing CRMs through approved integrations, APIs, webhooks, or similar connections. Over time, MMS may consolidate fragmented advisor-facing third-party capabilities, including CRM functionality, without replacing Tinman as the core loan workflow.

Rollout sequence: Complete and measure the NEO rollout. Next, bring MMS to the required level of enterprise readiness and roll it across Better, including Better.com DTC. Then extend the platform to Better Wholesale as a sign-up, retention, and funded-loan tool. Future external clients follow once those internal deployments validate the product, operating model, integrations, and economics.

Development pipeline: Following V1, the product roadmap should prioritize enhancements that deepen user adoption and measurable impact, including broader workflow automation, richer client and partner experiences, additional integrations, reporting and operating insights, AI capabilities, and enterprise readiness. Each initiative should be prioritized against the investment filter and the evidence produced in V1.

02 · INTERNAL ECONOMICS

Use MMS to make Better Wholesale more valuable

NEO subscription: Full MMS access is $249 per eligible Mortgage Advisor per month. All Mortgage Advisors are included in the NEO adoption model. Support staff do not pay a user fee.

Wholesale access model: MMS should be a recruitment and relationship tool for Better Wholesale, giving brokers best-in-class tools to support their business. Brokers receive MMS at no charge while they maintain an active Better Wholesale account. Better Wholesale funds the MMS division $49 per funded loan. This keeps the broker experience free while directly aligning MMS funding with funded production.

The definitive program should define an active account, the billing period, the funded-production measure, and the internal funding mechanics.

Better option: Better's internal use, including Better.com DTC, may likewise be priced at $49 per funded loan as a success fee, billed to the applicable client as part of Better's fee set. This aligns MMS cost with funded production and gives the relevant Better cost center a lower-risk path to adoption.

Addressable technology opportunity: approximately $415,000 annually in identified third-party technology expense may be addressable by MMS. MMS already replaces Mortgage Coach, is nearing Lendware replacement, is expected to replace Home Report and List Reports, and may replace part of MBS Highway.

Savings discipline: Home Report replacement requires a data-aggregator connection, so that cost must be netted before savings are recognized. MBS Highway savings should remain partial until Better's enterprise agreement and daily-update requirements are confirmed. Savings should be recognized only when the underlying expense is actually eliminated, reduced, consolidated, or avoided.

MMS should have a clear internal P&L showing platform revenue, verified technology savings, development and operating expense, external revenue, and net financial contribution.

04 · INVESTMENT

Use a lean, fail-fast investment model

Better is not being asked to fund a separate software organization. Daryn owns the initial MMS build and business case. PDE provides the enterprise technical partnership needed to validate architecture, security, integrations, and deployment readiness.

Codex-assisted development can materially reduce the cost and time required to build, test, and improve MMS compared with traditional software development. The investment should remain disciplined: build quickly, test with real users, measure the result, scale what works, and stop what does not.

  • Codex and AI-development costs.
  • Incremental PDE, Product, Design, and Engineering resources.
  • Security, compliance, and legal review.
  • Hosting, APIs, infrastructure, and implementation costs.
  • Other direct costs specifically attributable to MMS.

NEO can also reduce the need for separate third-party vendors and for teams managing integrations between those tools and Tinman. When a third-party capability is strategically important, MMS can determine whether building it internally is faster, less expensive, and better integrated than continuing to license it.

Existing corporate overhead, pre-existing salaries, unrelated technology projects, and costs that would have existed without MMS should not automatically be allocated to MMS. The intent is to account for the actual investment in the platform without artificially burdening the business.

05 · ROLE

Vice President and General Manager

With MMS rolling out across NEO, Daryn would serve as Vice President, Client Experience & Strategic Ventures and General Manager, MyMortgageSTUDIO.

The role exists to serve NEO and Better first by improving the Mortgage Advisor, client, partner, and consumer experience. Future external clients are the expansion opportunity after internal value is proven.

This is an operating role, not an advisory assignment. Daryn owns the initial build, MMS vision, product roadmap, experience strategy, adoption, commercialization, and the operating result. PDE owns technical standards and clears MMS for deployment.

The role carries accountability for the Client Experience and MMS P&Ls and the authority to work across teams to change behavior, drive adoption, and deliver results.

  • Identify opportunities to replace or reduce third-party software expense.

06 · COMPENSATION

$20,000 monthly income floor and Better equity

Daryn would receive a minimum of $20,000 per month upon formal sponsorship of the VP and GM mandate, allowing Daryn to put MMS to work for NEO and Better. The income floor supports the role and is a non-recourse draw against Daryn’s 49% participation in MMS economic value.

As part of the VP compensation package, Daryn would also participate in Better’s equity program through an initial RSU or stock-option grant. The amount, vesting schedule, and terms would be determined through Better’s applicable equity plans and compensation practices.

The monthly income floor is credited against MMS participation. If the draw exceeds Daryn’s participation in a given month or period, Daryn has no personal repayment obligation. Better equity is compensation for the VP role and alignment with Better as a whole. MMS participation is separate and recognizes the creation, operation, and commercialization of the MMS asset.

07 · MMS PARTICIPATION

49% economic participation; 51% Better control

Daryn would receive a 49% economic participation right in MMS, with Better retaining 51% and control of the business. The participation applies to distributable MMS economics after agreed operating expenses and approved MMS-specific startup cost recovery.

The commercial intent is that the participation follows MMS at the same 49% level. If MMS is sold, spun off, transferred to a subsidiary, renamed, licensed, merged into another platform, or included in a larger transaction, Daryn’s participation continues to attach to the economic value attributable to MMS.

Documentation principle: Legal and Finance may select the appropriate structure, subsidiary, joint venture, contractual participation right, profits interest, or another mechanism, provided the agreed economic intent is preserved.
